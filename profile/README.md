## About NSSIL

The Neural Systems & Signal Intelligence Laboratory develops computational frameworks and foundation models for neural-signal analysis, with clinical brain–computer interfaces and AI-assisted neurological diagnostics at the centre.

Our work brings together signal processing, machine learning, computational neuroscience, information theory, and biologically inspired computing. We emphasize interpretable methods, careful validation, and reproducible research.

## Research areas

- **EEG foundation models and self-supervised learning** — pathology-aware pretraining and information-theoretic representation learning for EEG.
- **Brain–computer interfaces and assistive neurotechnology** — real-time EEG decoding, adaptive classification, communication, rehabilitation, and attention monitoring.
- **Chaos theory and neural dynamics** — nonlinear and dynamical-systems methods for characterizing neural signals.
- **Clinical neurological AI** — clinically grounded, multi-centre methods for epilepsy diagnosis and related neurological applications.
- **Biologically inspired neural architectures** — spiking neural networks, reservoir computing, and excitatory–inhibitory-balanced recurrent models.
- **Information theory and neural-signal fidelity** — rigorous measures of information preservation in neural-signal representations.
